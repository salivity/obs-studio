How to Lock Volume Sliders in OBS Studio

Accidental adjustments to your audio levels during a live stream or recording can ruin your production quality. Fortunately, OBS Studio includes a built-in feature that allows you to lock the volume sliders in the Audio Mixer. This quick guide will show you how to lock your audio levels in place, ensuring your stream's volume remains consistent and safe from accidental mouse clicks.

Steps to Lock Audio Sliders in OBS Studio

To secure your audio levels in the OBS Studio interface, follow these simple steps:

  1. Open OBS Studio and locate the Audio Mixer panel (usually positioned at the bottom of the window).
  2. Right-click on any empty space within the Audio Mixer dock, or right-click directly on one of the volume sliders.
  3. From the context menu that appears, click on Lock Volume.

Once selected, the volume sliders in your mixer will become locked in their current positions. You will no longer be able to drag them with your mouse, preventing any accidental changes during a broadcast.

How to Unlock the Volume Sliders

If you need to make adjustments to your audio levels later, you can easily disable the lock:

  1. Right-click in the empty space of the Audio Mixer panel.
  2. Click on Lock Volume to uncheck the option.
  3. Adjust your volume sliders to the desired levels, and then repeat the locking process to secure them again.

By keeping your volume sliders locked, you eliminate the risk of accidental audio drops or sudden volume spikes, ensuring a professional and consistent audio experience for your audience.
