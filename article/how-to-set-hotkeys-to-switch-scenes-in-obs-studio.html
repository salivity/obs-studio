How to Set Hotkeys to Switch Scenes in OBS Studio

Using hotkeys to switch scenes in OBS Studio is one of the best ways to stream or record video content smoothly without manually clicking around your screen. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to access your OBS settings, locate your created scenes, and assign custom keyboard shortcuts so you can transition between different camera angles, overlays, and gameplay screens instantly.

Step-by-Step Guide to Assigning Scene Hotkeys in OBS

Follow these simple steps to configure your scene transition shortcuts:

1. Open the OBS Settings Menu

Launch OBS Studio on your computer. In the bottom-right corner of the interface, click on the Settings button in the Controls dock. Alternatively, you can click on File in the top menu bar and select Settings from the drop-down menu.

2. Navigate to the Hotkeys Tab

In the Settings window that appears, look at the left-hand sidebar and click on Hotkeys. This will open a list of all controllable functions, sources, and scenes within OBS Studio.

3. Locate Your Scenes

Scroll down through the Hotkeys list. The list is organized alphabetically and by category. Scroll past the general OBS controls until you find the section corresponding to the specific scene you want to control. Each scene you have created in your main window will have its own dedicated section here.

4. Assign the Shortcut

Within your chosen scene's section, look for the field labeled Switch to scene.

  1. Click inside the empty text box next to Switch to scene.
  2. Press the key or combination of keys on your keyboard (for example, Ctrl + F1, Numpad 1, or a specific function key) that you want to use for this transition.
  3. If you make a mistake, you can click the trash can icon next to the input field to clear the shortcut and try again.

5. Apply and Save Your Changes

Repeat the process for any other scenes you want to control with your keyboard. Once you have finished assigning all of your hotkeys, click the Apply button in the bottom-right corner of the Settings window, and then click OK to close the menu.

6. Test Your Hotkeys

Return to the main OBS Studio interface. Press the hotkeys you just configured to ensure that OBS seamlessly switches between your designated scenes.
