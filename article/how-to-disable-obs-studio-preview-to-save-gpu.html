How to Disable OBS Studio Preview to Save GPU

Running OBS Studio can heavily tax your graphics card, especially when streaming or recording high-end games. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to completely disable the OBS Studio preview window to free up valuable GPU resources and improve your overall system performance.

Why Disable the OBS Preview?

When OBS Studio is open, it constantly renders your scene in real-time within the preview window. This rendering process requires active GPU cycle consumption. By disabling this preview, you stop the GPU from rendering the application interface's video feed, directing those saved hardware resources back into your game or other demanding applications.

Method 1: The Quick Right-Click Method

This is the fastest way to toggle the preview window off and on.

  1. Open OBS Studio.
  2. Locate the main preview window where your scene is being displayed.
  3. Right-click anywhere inside the preview window.
  4. From the context menu that appears, click to uncheck Enable Preview.

The preview screen will immediately go black and display the text "Preview disabled."

Method 2: Using the Top Menu Bar

If you prefer using the application menus, you can disable the preview from the top navigation bar.

  1. Open OBS Studio.
  2. Click on View in the top menu bar.
  3. Hover your mouse over the Preview option (if applicable in your version) or look directly for Enable Preview in the dropdown.
  4. Click Enable Preview to uncheck it.

Studio Mode Considerations

If you are using Studio Mode, OBS renders two separate preview windows: "Preview" and "Program."

  • To save the maximum amount of GPU resources, it is highly recommended to click the Studio Mode button on the right-hand control panel to turn it off completely before disabling the preview. Running Studio Mode inherently consumes more system resources.

How to Re-enable the Preview

If you need to adjust your overlays, crop a source, or check your framing, you can easily turn the preview back on:

  1. Right-click on the black "Preview disabled" screen.
  2. Click Enable Preview to check it again.
  3. Make your adjustments, and then disable it again using the same steps to keep saving GPU resources during your live stream or recording session.
