Map 8-Channel Audio to Stereo in OBS Studio

When an 8-channel (7.1 surround) discrete audio input is fed into OBS Studio configured for stereo output, the software relies on an automated, internal downmixing matrix based on standard ITU-R downmixing algorithms to condense the multi-channel feed into two channels. This article covers how OBS Studio handles discrete channel assignments, how it mathematically folds surround and center channels into left and right stereo outputs, and what tools you can use if you need granular control over the mapping process.

The Automatic Downmixing Matrix

OBS Studio automatically downmixes multichannel audio when the project's global audio setting is set to stereo under Settings > Audio > Channels. If a source delivers 8 discrete channels (standard 7.1 surround: Left, Right, Center, LFE/Subwoofer, Left Surround, Right Surround, Left Back, and Right Back), OBS applies a standardized summing algorithm:

  • Left (L) and Right (R): Channels 1 and 2 pass directly to their respective stereo outputs at full scale (0 dB).
  • Center (C): Channel 3 is split equally and folded into both the Left and Right stereo channels, typically attenuated by -3 dB to compensate for acoustic summing and maintain consistent perceived loudness.
  • Low-Frequency Effects (LFE): Channel 4 is either dropped or heavily attenuated depending on the underlying audio driver and operating system framework (such as CoreAudio or WASAPI) to prevent clipping and low-end muddiness in stereo mixes.
  • Surrounds and Rears (Ls, Rs, Lb, Rb): Channels 5 through 8 are summed into their corresponding Left and Right channels. They are attenuated (generally between -3 dB and -6 dB) to ensure background spatial information does not overpower primary front-stage elements.

Native Track Routing and Limitations

Under Edit > Advanced Audio Properties, OBS Studio allows users to assign inputs to six discrete recording/streaming tracks. When the global audio mode is configured to Stereo:

  • Each of the six tracks operates strictly as a two-channel stereo bus.
  • Checking a track box routes the already downmixed stereo sum of that source to the target track.
  • Selecting the "Downmix to Mono" checkbox forces the entire 8-channel sum into an identical, dual-mono signal sent to both stereo channels.

OBS Studio does not natively include an interactive matrix mixer in the core user interface. You cannot adjust individual discrete channel faders (such as attenuating channel 4 or soloing channel 3) using standard OBS properties alone.

Overriding the Default Mapping

If the automatic downmix causes phase issues, muddiness, or improper balance, you must use external routing or plugins to handle custom channel mapping:

  1. VST Plugins: Add a multichannel VST plugin (such as a matrix mixer, surround utility, or channel-strip plugin) to the source's Filters list. This allows you to mute, balance, or reposition specific discrete channels before the signal hits the OBS master output.
  2. OBS Plugins: Community tools like the OBS Audio Monitor or custom matrix mapper plugins expose discrete pin-mapping inside the mixer UI, letting you selectively route channels 1–8 directly to discrete virtual destinations.
  3. Virtual Audio Interfaces: Digital audio workstations (DAWs) or software mixers (such as Voicemeeter or Loopback) can accept the 8-channel hardware stream, perform the desired submixing, and deliver a clean two-channel output directly to OBS.
