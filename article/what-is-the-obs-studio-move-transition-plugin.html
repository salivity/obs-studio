What is the OBS Studio Move Transition Plugin?

The Move Transition plugin for OBS Studio is a popular add-on that allows broadcasters and video creators to smoothly animate transitions between scenes and individual sources. This article explains what the plugin is, how it works, and its primary features to help you enhance the visual appeal of your live streams and video recordings.

At its core, the Move Transition plugin—created by developer Exeldro—eliminates abrupt cuts and simple fades by animating the movement of elements on your screen. When you switch between scenes that share the same sources (such as a camera feed, browser source, or image), the plugin automatically calculates the difference in position, size, and rotation, and smoothly glides the source from its old position to its new one.

Beyond basic scene transitions, the plugin offers several powerful features:

  • Move Source Filter: This allows you to trigger the movement of specific sources within a single scene. You can set up hotkeys or stream deck commands to move, resize, or rotate a webcam or overlay on command.
  • Move Value Filter: This feature lets you animate properties of other filters, such as changing color correction, blur, or crop values over a specified duration.
  • Audio Triggering: You can link movements to audio input levels, meaning a source can automatically scale up, shake, or bounce when a microphone detects sound.

To use it, you simply install the plugin, add "Move" as a transition type in your OBS Scene Transitions dock, and configure the easing options to control how fast or smooth the movement feels. It is highly customizable, making it an essential tool for achieving a professional, dynamic, and broadcast-quality aesthetic in OBS Studio.
