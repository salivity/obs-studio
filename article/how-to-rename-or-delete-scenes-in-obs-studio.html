How to Rename or Delete Scenes in OBS Studio

Organizing your production layout is essential for a smooth live stream or recording session. This guide provides a direct, step-by-step walkthrough on how to quickly rename or delete existing scenes in OBS Studio, helping you keep your workspace clean and efficient.

How to Rename a Scene in OBS Studio

Renaming scenes helps you identify your camera angles, game captures, and transitions quickly during a live broadcast.

  1. Locate the Scenes dock, which is typically found in the bottom-left corner of the OBS Studio interface.
  2. Left-click on the scene you want to rename to highlight it.
  3. Right-click the highlighted scene to open the context menu.
  4. Select Rename from the list. Alternatively, you can select the scene and press the F2 key on your keyboard.
  5. Type your desired name for the scene and press Enter to save the changes.

How to Delete a Scene in OBS Studio

Removing unused scenes declutters your interface and reduces the risk of accidentally switching to an empty or outdated layout.

  1. Go to the Scenes dock in the bottom-left corner of OBS Studio.
  2. Left-click on the scene you wish to delete to select it.
  3. Use one of the following three methods to remove it:
    • Click the Minus icon (-) located at the bottom of the Scenes dock.
    • Right-click the scene and select Remove from the context menu.
    • Press the Delete key on your keyboard.
  4. A confirmation prompt will appear asking if you are sure you want to remove the scene. Click Yes to permanently delete it.
