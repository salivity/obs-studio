OBS Studio: Stream Landscape and Output Vertical Video

This article explains how to configure OBS Studio to broadcast a standard landscape stream to platforms like YouTube or Twitch while simultaneously outputting a dedicated 9:16 vertical video feed to a secondary display for digital signage. By utilizing specialized plugins, nested scenes, and hardware projection tools, content creators and event organizers can manage both aspect ratios independently within a single instance of OBS without requiring duplicate hardware.

Using the Aitum Vertical Plugin

The most effective method for simultaneous landscape and vertical output is the free Aitum Vertical plugin.

  1. Installation: Download and install the plugin from the official OBS forum or Aitum website. Once installed, it adds a dedicated vertical canvas (1080x1920) alongside your default 16:9 canvas.
  2. Scene Setup: Create vertical layouts using your existing sources or separate media designed specifically for the digital signage display.
  3. Routing the Vertical Feed: Instead of streaming the vertical feed online, open the Vertical Canvas settings and configure the Virtual Camera or use the Fullscreen Projector. Select the connected digital signage monitor to output the clean, full-screen vertical feed directly to the display.
  4. Broadcast Landscape: Configure your primary OBS settings with your standard landscape RTMP stream keys. Clicking "Start Streaming" will broadcast the landscape feed as usual without interfering with the vertical projection.

Using OBS Fullscreen Projector with Dedicated Scenes

If you prefer not to install third-party plugins, you can accomplish this natively by utilizing secondary scenes and display scaling.

  1. Create a Signage Scene: Build a dedicated scene in OBS composed specifically for your vertical signage. If your main canvas is set to 1920x1080, rotate your vertical media 90 degrees to fit the canvas, or keep it centered with a 9:16 aspect ratio.
  2. Assign the Output: Right-click the dedicated signage scene in the Scenes list, navigate to Fullscreen Projector (Scene), and select the monitor attached to your digital signage setup.
  3. Hardware Orientation: If the digital signage display is physically rotated (portrait mode), set the display orientation to "Portrait" in your operating system's display settings. OBS will scale the preview or projector window appropriately.

Dedicated Video Routing via NDI or DeckLink

For enterprise environments where the digital signage player is on a separate network device or requires SDI/HDMI distribution:

  1. Source Record Plugin: Use the Source Record plugin to tap directly into your vertical scene and route it to an auxiliary output such as a Blackmagic DeckLink card.
  2. OBS-NDI: Output the specific vertical scene as an NDI stream across your local network. A networked digital signage media player running an NDI receiver can capture and display the vertical feed with minimal latency while your primary canvas continues its standard RTMP broadcast.
