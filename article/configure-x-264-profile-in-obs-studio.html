Configure x264 Profile in OBS Studio

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to configure the x264 encoder profile in OBS Studio to either "Main" or "High" settings. You will learn the step-by-step process to locate this setting in your OBS interface and understand which profile to select to guarantee smooth video playback on your target mobile devices.

Understanding "Main" vs. "High" Profiles for Mobile

Before changing your settings, it is important to choose the profile that matches your audience's hardware:

  • Main Profile: This profile is ideal for older or lower-spec mobile devices. It requires less CPU power to decode, ensuring that older smartphones and tablets can play your stream or video without stuttering.
  • High Profile: This is the standard for modern mobile devices and computers. It offers superior compression efficiency and better visual quality at the same bitrate, but it requires more processing power to decode.

How to Change the x264 Profile in OBS Studio

Follow these steps to configure the encoder profile in OBS Studio:

  1. Open Settings: Launch OBS Studio and click on the Settings button located in the bottom-right corner of the interface (under the "Controls" dock).
  2. Access Output Settings: In the left-hand menu of the Settings window, click on Output.
  3. Switch to Advanced Mode: At the top of the Output settings page, locate the Output Mode dropdown menu and change it from "Simple" to Advanced.
  4. Select Your Tab: Depending on what you are configuring, click on either the Streaming or Recording tab at the top.
  5. Set Encoder to x264: In the Encoder dropdown menu, ensure that x264 is selected.
  6. Adjust the Profile: Scroll down to the encoder settings section. Locate the Profile dropdown menu (it is often set to "none" by default).
    • Select main if you want to prioritize compatibility for older mobile devices.
    • Select high if you want to prioritize video quality for modern mobile devices and standard players.
  7. Save Changes: Click the Apply button in the bottom-right corner, and then click OK to close the settings window.
