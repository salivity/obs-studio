Configure Multi-Channel SDI Audio in OBS Studio

Capturing multi-channel embedded SDI audio in OBS Studio allows broadcast operators and production teams to separate camera microphones, talent comms, and ambient feeds into discrete, mixable tracks. This guide outlines the step-by-step process of preparing your capture hardware, configuring OBS Studio's internal audio channel capacity, adding the SDI source, and mapping discrete audio channels for live mixing, recording, and streaming.

1. Prepare Capture Hardware and Drivers

Before configuring OBS Studio, verify that your SDI capture device (such as an AJA Kona, Blackmagic DeckLink, or Magewell Pro Capture card) is configured to pass multi-channel embedded SDI audio.

  1. Open your capture card’s vendor utility (e.g., Blackmagic Desktop Video Setup or Magewell USB Capture Utility).
  2. Locate your SDI input settings and confirm that the audio input is set to Embedded SDI.
  3. Ensure the hardware is configured to recognize up to 8 or 16 channels of embedded audio, rather than downmixing to stereo at the driver level.

2. Set Global Audio Channels in OBS Studio

By default, OBS Studio processes audio in Stereo (2 channels). To ingest more than two channels, you must expand the internal audio canvas.

  1. Launch OBS Studio and navigate to Settings > Audio.
  2. Under the General section, locate the Channels dropdown.
  3. Change the setting from Stereo to 4.0, 5.1, or 7.1 depending on how many discrete channels your workflow requires (7.1 supports up to 8 distinct channels).
  4. Click Apply and restart OBS Studio when prompted to apply the new audio architecture.

3. Add the SDI Capture Source

Bring the video and embedded audio into OBS via the dedicated capture source.

  1. In the Sources dock, click the + (Add) icon.
  2. Select your device source:
    • For Blackmagic hardware, select Blackmagic Device for direct driver integration.
    • For other cards (AJA, Magewell, etc.), select Video Capture Device.
  3. Select your SDI capture card from the Device list.
  4. Set your standard video resolution and frame rate to match your camera output.
  5. In the audio configuration area:
    • If using a Blackmagic source, check the audio settings to ensure multi-channel is enabled.
    • If using a standard Video Capture Device, check Use custom audio device if the audio does not automatically latch, and select the corresponding SDI audio device.
  6. Click OK.

4. Route and Map Channels in Advanced Audio Properties

Once the source is active, OBS receives the embedded channels mapped across its surround matrix (Front Left, Front Right, Center, LFE, Surround Left, Surround Right, etc.).

  1. Locate your source in the Audio Mixer dock.
  2. Click the gear icon next to the source or right-click anywhere in the Audio Mixer and select Advanced Audio Properties.
  3. Under the Tracks columns (1 through 6), select which master tracks will receive the audio.
    • For live streaming, track 1 is typically the primary output.
    • For ISO recording, map separate feeds across tracks 1 through 6 under Settings > Output > Recording.

5. Splitting Channels for Independent Mixing (Optional)

If your camera embeds distinct mono signals (e.g., Channel 1: Boom, Channel 2: Lav, Channel 3: Director Comms) that require individual volume faders and filters, use channel filtering:

  1. Right-click the SDI source in the Sources dock and select Filters.
  2. Under Audio Filters, add a Gain or third-party downmix/matrix plugin.
  3. Alternatively, duplicate the capture source (or use audio-only virtual devices) and apply channel panning or matrix plugins (such as Audio Monitor or obs-matrix-filter) to isolate specific SDI channel pairs onto separate mixer faders.
