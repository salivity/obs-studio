Import Streamlabs Scenes into OBS Studio

Transitioning from Streamlabs to OBS Studio is a common move for creators looking for better performance and customization. This article provides a straightforward guide on how to import your Streamlabs scene collections directly into OBS Studio using the software's built-in import tool, ensuring a seamless migration of your overlays, scenes, and sources.

How to Import Streamlabs Scenes into OBS Studio

OBS Studio includes a native import tool that automatically detects Streamlabs (Streamlabs Desktop) installations on your computer and allows you to copy your scenes over in just a few clicks.

Step-by-Step Migration Guide

  1. Open OBS Studio on your computer.
  2. In the top menu bar, click on Scene Collection.
  3. Select Import from the dropdown menu.
  4. A window will appear showing a list of detected scene collections from other streaming software. OBS Studio automatically scans your system for Streamlabs data.
  5. Find the Streamlabs scene collection you wish to transfer and check the box next to it.
  6. Click the Import button at the bottom of the window.
  7. To activate your imported scenes, go back to the top menu, click Scene Collection, and select your newly imported Streamlabs collection from the list.

Important Considerations After Importing

While the import tool transfers the majority of your layout, certain elements may require manual adjustment:

  • Browser Sources (Alerts and Widgets): Streamlabs-specific widgets (like alert boxes, chat overlays, and goal bars) will transfer as browser sources. Ensure these sources are still active and log into your Streamlabs dashboard to copy/paste the correct widget URLs if they appear blank.
  • Audio Sources: You may need to reconfigure your microphone and desktop audio capture settings within OBS Studio, as audio device routing can sometimes reset during the import process.
  • Local Files: Ensure that any local video files, images, or stinger transitions used in your Streamlabs scenes remain in their original folder paths on your computer so OBS Studio can locate them.
