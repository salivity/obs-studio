How to Stream 1080p 60fps on Facebook Gaming OBS

Yes, you can stream directly to Facebook Gaming at 1080p resolution and 60 frames per second (fps) using OBS Studio. Doing so requires a stable high-speed internet connection, capable hardware, and specific encoder configurations in your streaming software. However, access to 1080p 60fps streaming is generally restricted to creators who are part of the Facebook Gaming Level Up Program or are official Facebook Partners. Standard accounts are typically limited to 720p at 30fps.

Facebook Gaming Level Up Program Requirement

Before configuring your settings, you must unlock 1080p 60fps privileges. To join the Level Up program, your gaming creator page must meet the following criteria:

  • Have at least 100 page followers.
  • Stream gaming content for at least 4 hours in the prior 14 days.
  • Stream gaming content on at least 2 days in the prior 14 days.
  • Stream from an eligible country and comply with Facebook's community standards and monetization policies.

Required OBS Studio Settings for 1080p 60fps

Once you have unlocked the feature, apply the following settings in OBS Studio to ensure your stream meets Facebook’s strict ingestion requirements.

1. Video Settings

Navigate to Settings > Video in OBS:

  • Base (Canvas) Resolution: 1920x1080 (This matches your monitor's resolution or your game's resolution).
  • Output (Scaled) Resolution: 1920x1080 (This is the final resolution sent to Facebook).
  • Downscale Filter: Lanczos (Sharpened scaling, 36 samples) or Bicubic.
  • Common FPS Values: 60.

2. Output (Streaming) Settings

Navigate to Settings > Output. Set the Output Mode at the top to Advanced, then select the Streaming tab:

  • Audio Track: 1
  • Encoder: Select NVIDIA NVENC H.264 (if you have an Nvidia graphics card) or x264 (if you are relying on your CPU).
  • Rate Control: CBR (Constant Bitrate is mandatory for stable live streaming).
  • Bitrate: 4000 Kbps to 6000 Kbps (Facebook's maximum supported bitrate for 1080p 60fps is 9000 Kbps, but 6000 Kbps is optimal for viewer compatibility and stability).
  • Keyframe Interval: 2 (Facebook requires a keyframe interval of exactly 2 seconds).
  • Preset: Quality or Max Quality (for NVENC) / veryfast or faster (for x264).
  • Profile: high.

3. Audio Settings

Navigate to Settings > Audio:

  • Sample Rate: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz.
  • Audio Bitrate (under the Output > Audio tab): 128 Kbps (Facebook's maximum supported audio bitrate).

Hardware and Bandwidth Requirements

To maintain a smooth 1080p 60fps stream without dropped frames, your system and network must meet the following benchmarks:

  • Upload Speed: A minimum upload speed of 10 Mbps is recommended. Your stream bitrate of 6000 Kbps (6 Mbps) requires headroom for game traffic and background processes.
  • Connection Type: Always use a wired Ethernet connection rather than Wi-Fi to prevent bitrate fluctuations and packet loss.
  • Hardware: A dedicated GPU (Nvidia GTX 1660/RTX series or equivalent) or a multi-core processor (Intel i7/AMD Ryzen 7 or higher) to handle simultaneous gaming and video encoding.
