How to Dock Twitch Chat and Mod Tools in OBS Studio

Integrating Twitch chat and moderation panels directly into the modular OBS Studio interface allows creators to monitor interactions and manage their community without toggling between multiple browser tabs. This setup utilizes OBS Studio's native Custom Browser Docks feature, which turns any web-based URL into a moveable, resizable UI element that snaps directly into your broadcast workspace.

Step 1: Obtain Your Twitch Pop-out URLs

To dock these tools, you need the standalone web addresses for your Twitch chat and Mod View panels:

  1. Twitch Chat: Go to your Twitch channel page in a web browser. Click the gear icon at the bottom of the chat column and select Popout Chat. Copy the URL from the newly opened window (formatted as https://www.twitch.tv/popout/YOUR_USERNAME/chat?popout=).
  2. Twitch Mod View: Navigate to your channel's moderation dashboard at https://www.twitch.tv/moderator/YOUR_USERNAME and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

Step 2: Add Custom Browser Docks in OBS Studio

Once you have the URLs, integrate them into OBS Studio:

  1. Launch OBS Studio.
  2. Click on the Docks menu located in the top toolbar.
  3. Select Custom Browser Docks... from the dropdown menu.
  4. In the Dock Name column, type a descriptive label (such as "Twitch Chat" or "Mod View").
  5. In the corresponding URL column, paste the URL you copied in Step 1.
  6. Click Apply, then click Close.

Step 3: Authenticate with Twitch

A new, floating window will immediately appear inside OBS Studio containing the webpage you specified.

  • If prompted, click Log In inside the dock window to enter your Twitch credentials.
  • Complete any required two-factor authentication.
  • Once logged in, the dock will persist across your future OBS Studio sessions without requiring repeated sign-ins.

Step 4: Position and Lock the Docks

You can now arrange the docks anywhere within the OBS modular layout:

  1. Click and hold the top title bar of the new floating dock.
  2. Drag the window toward any side, corner, or existing panel inside OBS Studio.
  3. When a translucent blue highlight appears indicating a valid snap location, release the mouse button to lock the dock into place.
  4. Hover your cursor over the borders between panels to resize them to your preferred dimensions.
  5. To prevent accidental moving, go to the top menu, select Docks, and check Lock Docks.
