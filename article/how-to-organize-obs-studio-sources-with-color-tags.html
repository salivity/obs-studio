How to Organize OBS Studio Sources with Color Tags

Managing a busy stream layout in OBS Studio can quickly become overwhelming as your list of sources grows. This article provides a quick, step-by-step guide on how to organize your OBS Studio sources using color tags, helping you visually categorize your inputs, media, and overlays for a much smoother broadcasting workflow.

Step-by-Step Guide to Applying Color Tags

Color tags in OBS Studio allow you to highlight specific sources in your list, making them easier to identify at a glance. To apply a color tag:

  1. Locate your Source: In the Sources dock at the bottom of your OBS Studio screen, find the source you want to organize.
  2. Open the Context Menu: Right-click on the selected source.
  3. Select Set Color: Move your cursor down to the Set Color option in the dropdown menu.
  4. Choose Your Color: Select one of the pre-defined colors (such as Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, or Grey) from the list, or click Custom Color to choose a unique hue.

Once selected, the background of that source in your Sources dock will immediately change to the chosen color.

Best Practices for Color Coding

To get the most out of color tags, establish a consistent color scheme for your stream assets. Here is a recommended setup:

  • Red: Live cameras and video capture devices.
  • Green: Audio inputs and outputs (microphones, desktop audio).
  • Blue: Static images and background graphics.
  • Yellow: Interactive browser sources, alerts, and chat overlays.
  • Purple: Game capture and window capture sources.

How to Remove or Change Color Tags

If you want to change your organization structure, you can easily clear or modify your color tags:

  1. Right-click the colored source in the Sources dock.
  2. Hover over Set Color.
  3. Select Clear to remove the color completely, or choose a new color to update it.

By using these steps to color-code your sources, you can drastically reduce the time spent searching for specific layers during a live broadcast.
