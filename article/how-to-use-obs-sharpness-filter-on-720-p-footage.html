How to Use OBS Sharpness Filter on 720p Footage

Upscaling 720p capture card footage to a 1080p or 4K canvas in OBS Studio often results in a soft, blurry image. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to apply and configure the "Sharpen" filter in OBS Studio to restore image clarity and clean up your upscaled video feed without introducing distracting visual artifacts.

Why Upscaled 720p Footage Looks Blurry

When OBS Studio stretches a 720p video source to fit a larger resolution canvas (like 1080p), it uses scaling algorithms to fill in the missing pixels. This process naturally softens edges and dilutes fine details, especially in fast-moving gameplay or text. Applying a sharpening filter helps redefine these edges, making the upscaled image look crisp and closer to native high definition.

Step-by-Step: Applying the Sharpen Filter

Follow these steps to add the sharpening filter to your capture card source:

  1. Open OBS Studio and locate your capture card under the Sources dock.
  2. Right-click on your capture card source and select Filters from the context menu.
  3. In the Filters window, locate the Effect Filters section at the bottom-left.
  4. Click the plus (+) icon and select Sharpen from the list.
  5. Name the filter (e.g., "720p Sharpening") and click OK.

Finding the Best Sharpness Settings

Once the filter is added, you will see a single slider labeled Sharpness. Finding the right balance is crucial, as over-sharpening will introduce "ringing" artifacts, jagged edges, and digital noise.

  • Default Setting (0.08): This is a safe starting point, but it may be too subtle for heavily upscaled 720p footage.
  • Recommended Range (0.10 to 0.20): For most 720p capture cards upscaled to 1080p, a value within this range offers the best balance. It tightens the edges of text and textures without making the image look pixelated or grainy.
  • How to Adjust: Move the slider to 0.00 and slowly drag it to the right while watching a high-detail scene in your OBS preview window. Stop dragging once text and fine details become readable but before halo outlines (white glows around dark edges) begin to appear.

Complementary Setting: Scale Filtering

Before relying solely on the Sharpen filter, ensure your source scaling method is set correctly to give the filter the best possible baseline image:

  1. Right-click your capture card source in the source list.
  2. Hover over Scale Filtering.
  3. Select Bicubic or Lanczos.

Bicubic is generally best when combined with a Sharpen filter, as it provides a smooth upscale that the filter can then cleanly sharpen. Avoid "Point" scaling, as it will make the upscaled 720p footage look blocky and pixelated.
