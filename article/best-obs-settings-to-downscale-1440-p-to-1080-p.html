Best OBS Settings to Downscale 1440p to 1080p

Streaming in 1080p while gaming at a native 1440p resolution is a popular configuration for modern broadcasters. This guide details the most effective settings in OBS Studio to downscale your 1440p canvas to a 1080p stream output while preserving maximum image sharpness. By configuring your video canvas, selecting the correct downscale filter, and adjusting your bitrate, you can eliminate the blurriness often associated with resolution downscaling.

1. Configure the Video Tab (The Right Way to Downscale)

To get the sharpest downscaled image, you should perform the resolution change in the Video tab of OBS Studio rather than the Output tab. Downscaling in the Video tab uses your GPU's dedicated scaling pipelines, which yields a cleaner result and consumes fewer resources.

  1. Open OBS Studio and go to Settings -> Video.
  2. Set the Base (Canvas) Resolution to 2560x1440 (your native monitor resolution).
  3. Set the Output (Scaled) Resolution to 1920x1080 (the resolution your viewers will see).
  4. Uncheck "Rescale Output" in the Output tab settings, as doing it twice causes severe quality loss.

2. Select the Best Downscale Filter

Under the same Video tab, the Downscale Filter dropdown determines the algorithm OBS uses to compress the 1440p frame into a 1080p frame.

  • Lanczos (Sharpened scaling, 36 samples): This is the best choice for maximum sharpness. It preserves fine details, text, and sharp edges. Choose this if you have a mid-to-high-end graphics card.
  • Bicubic (Sharpened scaling, 16 samples): If Lanczos causes performance issues or minor visual artifacts (like ringing around text), Bicubic is the perfect alternative. It is slightly softer than Lanczos but offers a great balance of sharpness and performance.

3. Disable Encoder-Level Rescaling

In the Settings -> Output tab (with Output Mode set to Advanced), ensure that the Rescale Output checkbox under the Streaming tab is disabled.

If this box is checked, your encoder (like NVIDIA NVENC or AMD AMF) will perform a second downscaling process. This secondary scale bypasses the high-quality Lanczos/Bicubic filters, resulting in a significantly muddier and pixelated stream.

4. Increase Your Bitrate to Retain Sharpness

A perfect downscaling configuration can still look blurry if your bitrate is too low. High-motion games require more data to keep the downscaled image sharp.

  • For Twitch: Set your bitrate to 6,000 Kbps (the soft limit for most creators) or up to 8,000 Kbps if your internet connection and viewer transcoding allow it.
  • For YouTube: Set your bitrate between 9,000 Kbps and 12,000 Kbps for a flawless 1080p 60fps stream.

5. Fine-Tune Encoder Presets

Using the NVIDIA NVENC H.264 encoder is highly recommended for Nvidia GPU users. To ensure the downscaled 1080p output remains crisp during fast motion, use these settings in the Output tab:

  • Rate Control: CBR (Constant Bitrate)
  • Preset: P6: Slower (Better Quality) or P7: Slowest (Best Quality)
  • Tuning: High Quality
  • Multipass Mode: Two Passes (Quarter Resolution) or Two Passes (Full Resolution)
  • Profile: high
  • Look-ahead: Disabled
  • Psycho Visual Tuning: Enabled (This redirects bitrate to areas with high motion to prevent blurring)
